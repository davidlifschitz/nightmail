Nightmail

One card in hand. One sealed dispatch to deliver. Guess, peek, compare, and outlast the rival couriers on the night line — without ever letting go of the dispatch.

Round 1 Deck – Set aside –

Your turn

Drawn. Play one card.

How to play

You hold one card and draw a second each turn, then play one and resolve it. If you are the last courier standing — or hold the highest rank when the deck runs out — you take the round and a token. Most tokens wins the match.

Fine print

The Lantern Bearer protects you from other couriers' effects until your next turn (you may still inspect yourself). If you ever hold the Stoker with the Switchman or the Night Dispatch, you must discard the Stoker. Discarding the Night Dispatch for any reason eliminates you. With two couriers, three cards are set aside face-up at the start of each round. Tied showdowns award no token.

Source provenance

Original engine, roles, names, flavor, and presentation. The draw-one-play-one elimination skeleton follows the single-card-hand deduction genre; no upstream assets or source files are copied.
